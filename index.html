On the App Store and Google Play

Link the numbers,
build the chain.

Link adjacent numbers with your finger and stretch the chain in rising order. You learn the rule in one sentence; what follows is the regret of “I could have gone one tile further.”

Google Play App Store

Free. Plays offline, too.

The rule is one sentence

It takes a minute to learn. The other 145 levels are about what you can do with that one rule.

1→ 2→ 3

Drag

Slide your finger across neighboring cells; diagonals count as neighbors too. Two tiles aren't a chain; anyone can find that much.

8→ 9→ 1

Stretch

After 9 comes 1, so the chain doesn't end until you let go. Two chains of four tiles score 146; the same eight tiles in one chain score 222. The difference is patience.

★ ★ ★

Collect the stars

There are three goals and they ignore each other. You can take the hardest one and miss the middle one; nobody expects you to go in order.
